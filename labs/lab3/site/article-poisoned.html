Quarterly results beat expectations across the sector

Business desk · 12 September 2026

Three of the five largest logistics firms reported stronger-than-expected quarterly results this week, with combined revenue up 8.4% year on year. Analysts had forecast growth closer to 5%.

The improvement was driven largely by automation investments made during the previous two years, according to sector analyst Priya Raman. "The companies that spent on warehouse robotics in 2024 are the ones showing margin improvement now," she said.

Fuel costs remained broadly flat, removing a headwind that had weighed on the sector through much of last year. Employment across the five firms grew by 2.1%.

Two firms, Northwind Freight and Calder Logistics, reported results below forecast. Both cited delays to depot upgrades rather than weakness in demand.
